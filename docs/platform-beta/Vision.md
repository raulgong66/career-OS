# Platform Beta Vision

Platform Beta is the evolution of CareerOS from an AI document generation platform into a **Professional Knowledge Platform**.

## Strategic Shift

Platform Alpha answered the question: *"How do we generate tailored career documents from a structured profile?"*

Platform Beta answers the question: *"How do we continuously acquire, reason over, and evolve a professional's knowledge so that every career decision and every artifact is grounded in evidence?"*

The center of gravity moves from **document generation** to:

- **Continuous acquisition** - the professional knowledge base grows incrementally and stays current.
- **Reasoning** - deterministic analysis turns knowledge into insight (gaps, strengths, trajectory, readiness).
- **Knowledge evolution** - the canonical profile becomes a living model that is merged, reconciled, and enriched over time.
- **Intelligent career management** - analytics and opportunity matching turn the profile into an active career asset rather than a static resume.

## Principles Driving the Vision

1. The Canonical Professional Profile is the single source of truth and remains the foundation of everything.
2. Evidence always outweighs assumptions. Nothing is asserted without traceability.
3. Determinism is preserved. LLMs enrich and assist; they never decide alone.
4. Recruiter-facing output never exposes internal knowledge or metadata.
5. Every capability strengthens the Professional Knowledge Platform vision.

## North Star

By the end of Platform Beta, a professional should be able to:

- Import any career artifact (CV, certificate, project, reference) and have the profile **evolve automatically** with conflict detection and human review.
- See a **reasoned view** of their career: strengths, gaps, trajectory, and readiness - each claim backed by evidence.
- Receive **evidence-grounded recommendations** and opportunity matches, not generic suggestions.
- Generate **any career document** from a single canonical profile, confident that every line traces to verified knowledge.
- Maintain their professional knowledge over a **long-term memory** that accumulates and compounds across time.

## What Does Not Change

- The canonical profile schema and its role as source of truth.
- The deterministic reasoning engine as the primary decision layer.
- The artifact generator architecture and tailoring pipeline delivered in Platform Alpha.
- The guarantee that recruiter-facing documents stay clean of internal metadata.
